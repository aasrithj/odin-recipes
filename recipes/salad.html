Salad

Description

This tutorial instructs determined individuals on how to make a delicious salad from scratch. Follow along with the steps listed below to enjoy a yummy and fresh salad!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed greens
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cucumber, sliced
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup feta cheese, crumbled
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the mixed greens.
  2. Chop the cherry tomatoes, cucumber, and red onion.
  3. In a large mixing bowl, combine the chopped vegetables and feta cheese.
  4. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, balsamic vinegar, salt, and pepper.
  5. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss to combine.
