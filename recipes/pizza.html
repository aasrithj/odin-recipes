Pizza

Description

This tutorial instructs determined individuals on how to make a delicious pizza from scratch. Follow along with the steps listed below to enjoy a yummy and savory pizza!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 packet active dry yeast
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. In a small bowl, combine warm water and yeast. Let it sit for 5 minutes until it becomes frothy.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the yeast mixture, sugar, olive oil, flour, and salt. Mix until a dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 5-7 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  4. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover it with a damp cloth, and let it rise for 1-2 hours until it doubles in size.
  5. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C). Roll out the dough to your desired thickness and place it on a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  6. Add your favorite sauce, cheese, and toppings.
  7. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly.
  8. Remove from the oven, let it cool for a few minutes, slice, and enjoy!
